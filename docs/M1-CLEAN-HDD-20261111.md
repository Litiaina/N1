2. 8,192 fresh named uploads through M1 → native N1 LINK, both engines killed and
   reopened, offline shard census and every original version/payload verified.
3. All 24 previously selected physical service cases: metadata loss/restore,
   interrupted/durable upload recovery, permissions/quotas, archives, GC,

   Accel, TLS and object-size policy.

   is Procyon-backed M1; N1, durability and workload settings stay identical.

The guard monitors new kernel transport/filesystem errors as well as device and
mount identity. Any new fault stops the task's cleanup/test process; a stopped
campaign cannot produce its final `PASSED` marker. Each completed phase retains
its own evidence. Unrelated processes are not test targets.
